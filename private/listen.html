The Listen Editor

Clean up A-roll audio by reviewing and choosing sound inconsistencies in a timeline editor.

Compare the result

Listen to the original and changed passage at a similar volume. Judge the words and the character of the voice, as well as the loudness.

The recording determines which corrections are possible. Some need separate voice and music tracks; each edit below explains what to check.

How to review and apply suggestions

Edits

Raise quiet speech CorrectionStep::Gain.execute_correction

Raise a quiet reply so it sits comfortably in the conversation.

Background noise rises too. Check that the extra volume helps, and preserve intentional whispers.

Raise quiet speech findings
NameDescription
Quiet voicequiet_voice

Speech is difficult to hear.

Example: Quiet reply → Louder reply.

Balance voice levels CorrectionStep::Level.execute_correction

Bring quiet and loud passages closer in volume, reducing the need to adjust playback.

The adjustment is fixed within each selected passage. Check that emphasis and quieter moments still feel natural.

Balance voice levels findings
NameDescription
Uneven voice levelsuneven_voice_levels

Volume changes distract listeners or make some passages difficult to hear.

Example: Quiet and loud passages → Closer listening levels.

Control loud peaks CorrectionStep::LimitPeaks.execute_correction

Restrain sudden peaks while keeping the surrounding speech natural.

This cannot restore sound already distorted during recording.

Control loud peaks findings
NameDescription
Excessive peaksexcessive_peaks

Sudden loud sounds are uncomfortable or overload the output.

Example: Sudden loud burst → Restrained peak.

Center one-sided dialogue CorrectionStep::Map.execute_correction

When dialogue plays in one ear and the other side is silent, centre the voice for more comfortable listening.

If each side carries a different speaker, keep both voices.

Center one-sided dialogue findings
NameDescription
Dialogue on only one channelone_sided_dialogue

Voice plays predominantly through one ear.

Example: Voice on the left, silence on the right → Voice in both ears.

Reduce electrical hum CorrectionStep::ReduceHum.execute_correction

Reduce steady electrical hum underneath the voice.

Check that lower voices still sound full and natural after the change.

Reduce electrical hum findings
NameDescription
Electrical humelectrical_hum

Buzzing distracts from or masks speech.

Example: Voice with hum → Voice with reduced hum.

Lower music under speech CorrectionStep::Duck.execute_correction

Lower the soundtrack where it covers the words, while keeping the voice at its existing level.

This needs separate voice and music tracks. Music is lowered across the selected passage; it does not automatically rise and fall between words.

Lower music under speech findings
NameDescription
Music masks dialoguemusic_masks_dialogue

Listeners struggle to understand the words.

Example: Music covers an answer → Music lowered over that passage.

Smooth the music ending CorrectionStep::Fade.execute_correction

Fade the music into the ending. Keep a hard stop when it belongs to the edit.

Replay the ending after other cuts to check that the fade still fits.

Smooth the music ending findings
NameDescription
Abrupt music endingabrupt_music_ending

The music stops suddenly and makes the ending sound unfinished.

Example: Music stops suddenly → Music fades at the ending.
