Fix Spelling

Review a spelling suggestion and choose the replacement that matches what the speaker meant.

Spelling suggestions to review
NameDescription
Spelling

Correct a misspelled word while preserving what the speaker said.

Example: “recieve” → “receive.”

Remove a passage cut_samples

Cut false starts, unnecessary repetition and empty waiting. Keep the words that establish facts, uncertainty and context.

Listen across both edges of a cut. A shorter passage still needs to sound natural and say what the speaker meant.

Passages to review for cutting
NameDescription
Fillerfiller

Remove empty hesitation when it adds no meaning.

Example: “We, um, begin.” → “We begin.”

Filler likefiller_like

Remove an unnecessary “like”; keep real comparisons.

Example: “It’s, like, ready.” → “It’s ready.”

Stutterrepeated_token

Review accidental repeated words while preserving emphasis.

Example: “The the plan.” → “The plan.”

False startfalse_start

Remove an abandoned start so the complete thought can follow.

Example: “We sh— we should go.” → “We should go.”

Hedgehedge

Shorten empty softeners while keeping genuine uncertainty.

Example: “A, sort of, square button.” → “A square button.”

Slatemeta_talk

Remove recording directions that do not belong in the cut.

Example: “Mic on? Here’s the result.” → “Here’s the result.”

Tangentrambling

Shorten a detour while keeping useful context.

Example: Setup, detour, result → Setup, result.

Tightentighten

Lose extra wording without changing the point.

Example: “At this point, we’re ready.” → “We’re ready.”

Questioninterviewer

Remove a question only when the answer makes sense on its own.

Example: “When?” / “We began in May.” → “We began in May.”

Unpaid promiseunpaid_promise

Review promises of material that does not appear in the video.

Example: “Chart next. Here’s why.” → “Here’s why.”

Stray wordword_outside_speech

Check text where nobody spoke. Correcting it should preserve the recording.

Example: Music with an invented word → Music retained, text reviewed.

Silencesilence

Review empty waiting; keep pauses that serve the conversation.

Example: Empty wait, then “Hello.” → “Hello.”

Cover a spoken detail apply_tone

Cover a spoken number or unwanted word with a tone while keeping the surrounding passage.

Bleeping is not available for every recording. A privacy flag alone hides nothing: listen to the result and check captions and visible text before sharing.

Spoken details to review
NameDescription
Privacy Concernsprivate_contact

Cover a spoken contact detail while keeping the rest of the passage.

Example: “Call [private number].” → “Call [tone].”

Profanityswear

Cover a word that does not suit your audience.

Example: “That [swear] launch.” → “That [tone] launch.”

Review and choose

Compare takes for accuracy, completeness and delivery. Keep the version that says what you intended.

Uncertain words, missing text and overlapping voices need listening. A flag identifies a review point; it does not supply missing speech or repair the recording.

Choices to review
NameDescription
Mishearduncertain_word

Listen again to confirm a word that may have been misheard.

Example: Written “Vasso” → Confirmed “Vasu”.

Unknown languageuncertain_language

Confirm the language before relying on the transcript.

Example: Unknown language → Language confirmed.

Hindi scriptscript_choice

Choose the script that suits your reader. A preference alone does not rewrite the transcript.

Example: Script undecided → Devanagari preference recorded.

Retakeretake

Compare takes and keep the complete, accurate version.

Example: Take A / take B → One complete take.

Restatementparaphrase

Keep one clear explanation without losing new details.

Example: The same point twice → One complete explanation.

Crosstalkoverlap

Review overlapping voices without losing either person’s contribution.

Example: Two voices together → Passage kept for review.

Hookhook

Review a recorded line that could make a useful opening. A suggestion alone does not move it.

Example: Current opening → Stronger recorded line proposed.

Titletitle_tags

Review a suggested title. A title note does not add an on-screen title.

Example: Untitled passage → Title saved as a note.

Missing wordsspeech_without_words

Listen where speech is missing from the transcript. Missing text does not mean silence.

Example: Transcript gap → Passage marked for listening.

Speaker name and reallocate rename_speaker / move_paragraph

Name a speaker across their paragraphs, or assign a single paragraph to the person who said it.

Speaker corrections change the labels. The recorded voices and timing stay the same.
